Sunny Cove privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026

  • Everything Sunny Cove keeps stays on this phone. We, the makers, can’t see any of it.
  • No accounts, no adverts, no tracking, and nothing sent to us or anyone else.
  • Children never type, record their voice or take photos.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Parent Mode.

Who we are

Sunny Cove is a wellbeing app for children aged 4 to 16 and their families, made by Dr Tan CT. This notice explains, in plain words, what the app keeps and what it doesn’t. It is the same in every country.

What Sunny Cove keeps — only on this phone

A parent or guardian sets the app up. It keeps:

  • Each child’s first name or nickname, birth year and age group
  • Their pet, its powers and the treasures they found
  • Mood check-ins (just the face picked each day)
  • Games, puzzles, calm tools and missions done, Sparks and levels
  • Time spent in Kid Mode each day, and the time limits you set
  • Your wellbeing checks and their results
  • Your Parent PIN, stored scrambled and locked by the phone
  • When your free trial started, whether you have the Cove Pass, and when you agreed to this notice

What we never collect

  • No accounts, email addresses or phone numbers
  • No location, contacts, photos, videos or voice recordings
  • No typing by children — they only tap pictures and buttons
  • No adverts, no analytics, no tracking tools from other companies
  • Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Sunny Cove has no server, so we never see your family’s information

Your phone’s own backup

Your phone may include app data in its own backup, kept in your own Google or Apple account (for example Android backup or iCloud backup), so Sunny Cove can come back if you change phones. That backup is controlled by you in your phone’s settings, not by us, and we can’t see it. Your Parent PIN is never included.

Read aloud

For young children, Sunny Cove can read words aloud using your phone’s built-in voice. Sunny Cove sends nothing to do this. Some phones let you choose voices that work over the internet — that is a phone setting outside Sunny Cove.

Buying the Cove Pass

The free trial and the Cove Pass are handled by Google Play or Apple’s App Store, under their own privacy policies. Buying happens only in Parent Mode, behind your PIN. The store tells us that a purchase happened (for our sales records), but never anything about your children. Refunds follow the store’s rules.

Links to other websites

The only links to other websites are the helplines in “Getting help, anywhere”, in Parent Mode. Sunny Cove asks before opening them in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children and teens

Sunny Cove is set up and managed by a parent or guardian. For teens (13 to 16), the weekly summary shows only how many days they checked in, not which faces they picked.

Your choices and control

  • See and change each child’s profile in Parent Mode
  • Remove one child and everything about them
  • Delete everything on this phone in Parent Mode → Privacy and data
  • Deleting the app also deletes everything it kept on this phone

Changes to this notice

If Sunny Cove ever changes what it keeps, we will update this notice and ask you to read and agree to it again in the app before carrying on.
